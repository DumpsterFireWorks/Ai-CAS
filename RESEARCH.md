# Research

Cross-project research and reusable lessons from AI-CAS are cataloged in the central private research library:

**`DumpsterFireWorks/Projects-research--library-`**

Use the library to discover durable corrective-action, AI workflow, evidence, governance, architecture, audit, experiment, and cross-project findings.

## Authority boundary

- This repository remains authoritative for current AI-CAS code, project status, decisions, and original artifacts.
- The research library is the cross-project discovery and preservation index. Cataloging research there does not activate implementation here.
- Original source paths and commits remain authoritative provenance.

## Cleanup rule

Before archiving or deleting this repository or any research-bearing branch, issue, PR, experiment, or document, verify that reusable research has been cataloged in `Projects-research--library-`.

Do not move credentials, proprietary operational data, private runtime data, or sensitive evidence into the research library.
